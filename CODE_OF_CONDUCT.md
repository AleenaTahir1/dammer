# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work and not the person, and do not harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, Aleena Tahir, through [GitHub](https://github.com/AleenaTahir1) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aleenatahir). Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do so.
